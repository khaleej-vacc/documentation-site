# 5. Appendix - Temporary Procedures

## 5.1 General Provisions

This document supplements the standard operating procedures for temporary procedures at OTHH.

The runway remediation phases for RWY 16L/34R have concluded. The previous phase-specific procedures are no longer applicable and have been superseded by the temporary departure instructions detailed below.

These procedures shall remain in effect while the associated NOTAMs are valid.

---

## 5.2 Temporary Departure Instructions

- Temporary departure instructions apply to all departures from OTHH as specified below. Controllers shall issue the applicable instruction according to the runway in use.
- The initial climb automatically set on the departure list should be disregarded.
- DCL for OTHH should not be used until the sector file is updated to include headings in the clearance. Look out for updates in the "Sectorfile-Updates" channel in our discord.

=== "Northerly Configuration (34s)"

    | Runway | Procedure | NOTAM | Temporary Departure Instruction |
    |--------|--------------|-------|---------------------------------|
    | RWY 34R | YAMEEN 1 | A1077/26 | Passing 500 ft, turn right **heading 020°**, climb to altitude **4000 ft**, expect to rejoin assigned SID. |
    | RWY 34L | LUSAIL 1 | A1080/26 | Passing 500 ft, **maintain runway heading**, climb to altitude **3000 ft**, expect to rejoin assigned SID. |

=== "Southerly Configuration (16s)"

    | Runway | Procedure | NOTAM | Temporary Departure Instruction |
    |--------|--------------|-------|---------------------------------|
    | RWY 16L | ZURDOS 1 | A1078/26 | Passing 500 ft, turn left **heading 120°**, climb to altitude **4000 ft**, expect to rejoin assigned SID. |
    | RWY 16R | WAKRAH 1 | A1079/26 | Passing 500 ft, **maintain runway heading**, climb to altitude **3000 ft**, expect to rejoin assigned SID. |
!!! warning
    The temporary procedures (e.g. YAMEEN 1) **__do not__** replace the conventional RNAV SIDs (e.g. TULUB1A). Please standardise yourself with the updated phraseology which may be found under section 5.3.

---

## 5.3 Departure Clearance Phraseology

- The applicable temporary departure instruction shall be included in the departure clearance in addition to the assigned SID.
- The heading and climb instruction in the example shall be amended to match the runway in use, as specified in Section 5.2.

!!! example
    **Pilot:** "Hamad Clearance Delivery, QTR1Y, Boeing 777-300ER, information A, stand C10, requesting FL210, to Dubai."

    **Controller:** "QTR1Y, Hamad Clearance Delivery, information A correct, cleared to Dubai via the ALSEM2E departure. When passing 500 ft, turn right heading 020 degrees, climb to altitude 4000 ft, expect to rejoin assigned SID, squawk 2612."

    **Pilot:** "Cleared to Dubai via the ALSEM2E departure. When passing 500 ft, turn right heading 020 degrees, climb to altitude 4000 ft, expect to rejoin assigned SID, squawk 2612, QTR1Y."

    **Controller:** "QTR1Y, readback correct, QNH 1012, report ready for pushback."
