### 1.1 Bahrain Central (Sector CH/CL/CS)
OBBB_X_CTR must ensure that arrivals into OTHH/OTBD are:

- FL270 or FL290 by GEXIM
- FL210 by GIRMO

All arrivals into Doha must be handed off spaced by at least 8NM or greater.

### 1.2 Doha Control North (Sector N1/N2)
- Arrivals into Doha will be transferred to Doha North Low at GIRMO FL210. Your responsibility is to clear traffic for the arrival only but not runway or expected approach type.
### 1.3 Doha Control South (Sector S)
- Arrivals into Doha via GEXIM shall be transferred to Doha South at FL270 or FL290. Your responsibility is to clear traffic for an arrival and descend traffic FL250 before a release to **Doha Radar North (DOH_R1_APP)**

![Doha FIR Sectors](../../../img/OT/Doha_FIR_Sectors.png#center)

### 2.1 Doha Radar North
- You handle arrivals from HAYYA, VEDED, ALKAN, and TOSNA.
- You handle departures from TULUB, LUBET, ALVEN, VAXIN, and ALSEM
- All arrivals are to be descended to 13000FT before a release to Doha Approach (DOH_A1/A2_APP)
- Traffic should be released no later than 5,000FT from its assigned level, to allow continuous climb or descent.
### 2.2 Doha Radar South
- You handle arrivals from TOVOX, LAEEB, ORLEK
- You handle departures from KUPRO, BUNDU, ULIKA, DATRI
- All arrivals are to be descended to 13000FT before a release to the relevant Doha Approach sector (DOH_AX_APP)
- Traffic should be released no later than 5,000FT from its assigned level, to allow continuous climb or descent.
### 2.3 Doha Approach West
- All departures are to be climbed to 12000FT before a release to the relevant Doha Radar sector (DOH_RX_APP)
- Arrivals on the western downwind are to be descended to 6000FT before a release to the relevant Doha Director sector (DOH_FX_APP). Ensure traffic are released no later than KIPIK.
- Arrivals are to be handed off to Doha Director at 190 knots unless otherwise coordinated.
### 2.4 Doha Approach East
- All departures are to be climbed to 12000FT before a release to the relevant Doha Radar sector (DOH_RX_APP)
- Arrivals on the eastern downwind are to be descended to 5000FT before a release to the relevant Doha Director sector (DOH_FX_APP). Ensure traffic are released no later than EGNUG.
- Arrivals are to be handed off to Doha Director at 190 knots unless otherwise coordinated.
### 2.5 Doha Director
- During the event, we will be operating under Independent Parallel Approaches. In the most optimal scenario, we will have both director positions online.
- Traffic on eastern downwind for runways 34R/16L are **not** to be turned until they are maintaining 2500FT.
- Traffic on the localiser should be spaced by at least 5.5NM on the localiser to sequence departures in between.
- Speed compression methods will be used: 190/10 DME, 160/4 DME. A380s are to be assigned 160/5 DME
- Doha Director West (DOH_F2_APP) is also responsible for OTBD traffic.
